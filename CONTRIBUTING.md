# Contributing

Thank you for suggesting an entry. These rules apply to every list in this family.

## The rules

- One entry per pull request.
- One line per entry, in the format `- [Name](url) - One sentence ending in a full stop.`
- Keep each section in alphabetical order by surname.
- The link goes to the thing itself (the form, the dataset, the tool, the community), not to a page about it.
- Write plain English. No marketing adjectives, and no em dashes.
- Do not add rankings or claims you cannot point to on the linked page.
- Run `npx awesome-lint` before you open the pull request. The same check runs in CI, along with a link check.

## What belongs here

- Public professional information only.
- A person is listed with a role at an organisation and a link to a public profile or an organisation page.
- Founders and leaders of the firms and tools in [awesome-ai-native-law-firms](https://github.com/HumblePaper/awesome-ai-native-law-firms) and [awesome-contract-negotiation](https://github.com/HumblePaper/awesome-contract-negotiation) are always welcome.
- No contact details: no email addresses, phone numbers or home addresses.
- One line per person.
- To remove yourself, open an issue or a pull request. It will be merged without question.
